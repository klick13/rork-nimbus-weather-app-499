# Nimbus Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

## Overview

Nimbus ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Nimbus weather app handles your information.

## Information We Collect

### Location Information
Nimbus uses your device's location to show hyper-local weather conditions, forecasts, radar, and alerts for your current area. This location data is used only to request weather information from our free, third-party weather data providers and is not stored or transmitted to our own servers.

### Device Information
We do not collect any persistent device identifiers, personal identifiers, or usage analytics. The app stores your saved locations and temperature-unit preference locally on your device using Android's built-in storage.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Weather forecasts**: Your location is sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve current conditions, hourly forecasts, and daily forecasts.
- **Radar and map layers**: Map tiles are loaded from CartoDB and radar imagery from RainViewer.
- **Geocoding**: City and zip code searches are handled by Open-Meteo and Zippopotam.
- **IP-based fallback**: If your device cannot determine a precise GPS location, we may use a public IP geolocation service to estimate your general area so the app can still display relevant weather.

## Data Storage

All weather data is requested in real time and cached only briefly on your device for performance. Your saved locations and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers or maintain a database of user information.

## Third-Party Services

Nimbus uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

- Open-Meteo (weather data and geocoding): https://open-meteo.com/
- RainViewer (radar imagery): https://www.rainviewer.com/
- CartoDB (map tiles): https://carto.com/
- Zippopotam (US zip code lookup): https://api.zippopotam.us/
- Public IP geolocation services (geojs.io, ipapi.co) are used only as a fallback when GPS is unavailable.

## Children's Privacy

Nimbus is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and reflected by an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Nimbus support page in the Google Play Store listing.
